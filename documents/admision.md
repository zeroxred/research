# Admission Requirement

**Likely applicable category:**

✅ **Requirement (1)**

> Applicants must either have a master's degree or expect to acquire the degree before entering the Doctoral Course.

**Not likely applicable:**

❌ **Requirement (7)** — Special admission route for applicants without a Master's degree but with at least 2 years of research experience recognized as equivalent.

❌ **Requirement (8)** — Individual admission qualification screening for applicants recognized as having Master's-equivalent academic ability.

Therefore, the preliminary screening required for categories (7) and (8) should not apply.
